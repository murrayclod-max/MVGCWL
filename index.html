📖 Captain's Guide

How to enter match results on the Mill Valley Golf Club Winter League site

1

Sign In as Captain

Tap ✏️ Enter Results in the top-left corner of the header (or via the More menu on mobile). You'll see a login screen.

UsernameFirst initial + last name, lowercase — e.g. ldornhofer
PasswordLast 7 digits of your registered phone number

🔒 Forgot your password? Tap "Forgot password?" on the login screen. Enter your username and you'll see a hint showing the last 4 digits of your registered number. You can then set a new password.

2

Select the Week

After signing in, use the Select Week dropdown to choose the week you are entering results for. The current week's matches will appear below.

You will see all 5 weekly matchups but can only enter results for the matchup involving your team. Other matchups will be grayed out and locked with a 🔒 icon.

3

Find Your Match

Your team's matchup will be highlighted with a green YOUR MATCH badge. The other matchups will be visible for reference but locked for editing.

Example: Luke Dornhofer (Birds captain) will see the Birds matchup fully editable and the other 4 matchups locked.
4

Enter Each Tier Result

For each of the 10 tiers, enter two things:

ResultThe score margin — e.g. 3&2, 1 up, 2&1, Halved, Even
WinnerSelect the winning player (or "Halved") from the dropdown

💡 If you only know the winner's name (no score), just type their last name — the system will record it as a "1 up" win. For example, typing Dornhofer means Dornhofer won 1 up.

5

Save Your Results

Once all 10 tier results are filled in, tap the gold Save Results ✓ button at the bottom of your matchup card. A confirmation toast will appear at the bottom of the screen.

⚠ You can update results after saving — just re-enter the corrected values and save again. The updated result will be marked as AMENDED in the Results log so the commissioner can see it was changed.

6

Sign Out When Done

Tap Sign Out in the top-right of the Enter Results page when you're finished. Your session will also automatically clear when you close the browser tab.

🏆 Scoring Reminder

  • Win = 1 point for the winning player's team
  • Halved / Even = 0.5 points each
  • Bonus point: the team with more total points from all 10 tier matches earns an extra +1 team point
  • Individual tier points count toward each player's promotion/relegation standing for next season
  • A 5&4 loss earns an entry on the Wall of Shame 💀
